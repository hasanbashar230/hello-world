# SongRise — Instagram Carousel Content Plan (32 ideas)

**Product truth used in every post (do not exaggerate beyond this):**
Record 60 seconds of your own singing → SongRise learns your tone and pitch → writes affirmation lyrics for your theme → gives you a fully produced song in YOUR voice in about 60 seconds. Two variations. Free 60-sec preview, no card. $2.99 one song / $7.99 month / $59.99 year. 10 themes. Pop at launch. Voice encrypted, never sold, never used for training, delete anytime.

**ICP:** women 22–45. College girl before placement. Working woman who is "fine" in office and crying in car. New mother. Woman after breakup / divorce. Woman who is doing everything for everyone and nothing for herself. She has tried journaling, she has tried Calm, she has downloaded an affirmation app and opened it four times.

**Voice rules for all copy:**
- Short sentences. Simple words. No big English.
- Say the real thing, not the pretty thing.
- One idea per slide. Slide 1 must stop the thumb in 1 second.
- Never say "unlock", "elevate", "journey", "transform your life", "manifest your dream reality".
- Affirmations always in first person, present tense, and believable. "I am learning to trust myself" lands better than "I am a goddess".

**Format standard (every carousel):**
Slide 1 = hook (max 8 words, big type). Slides 2–6 = one line each + small support line. Slide 7 = affirmation block. Slide 8 = soft CTA ("Free 60-second preview. No card.").
